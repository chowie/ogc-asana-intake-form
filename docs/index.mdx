Oneida Gospel Church staff use this form to send requests to the Deacon board.
You fill out a short form, answer a couple of clarifying questions from **Philip**
(an AI assistant), review the summary, and confirm. The request lands in the
Asana **OGC Deacons** project as a task in the **Inbox** section.

## What happens to a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Staff sign in with their own invited account, using an emailed link or
    Google. Sessions end after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Form">
    Request title and details, plus optional due date, assignee, and file.
    The submitter's name and email come from their account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Philip">
    Up to three plain-language clarifying questions, then a five-part summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Nothing is sent to Asana until the summary is confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Asana">
    A task is created in the OGC Deacons project, Inbox section. The
    submitter gets the link on screen and by email, and can follow the task
    in Asana for updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  No credentials ever reach the browser. Every secret (the Asana token, the
  Anthropic key, the email and Redis credentials) lives server-side in Netlify
  Functions. The session is an `HttpOnly` cookie that page scripts can't read.
</Note>
