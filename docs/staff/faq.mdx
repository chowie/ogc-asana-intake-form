## Getting in

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get an account?">
    Ask a deacon for an invitation. Accounts are invitation-only. The invite
    link works once, only for the email it was sent to, and expires after 48
    hours. See [Submitting a request](/staff/submitting-a-request#1-sign-in).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My invite link says it has expired or was already used">
    Invites expire after 48 hours and work once. If you registered but didn't
    confirm your email within 24 hours, the registration lapses too. Either
    way, ask the deacon who invited you to send a new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says my email doesn't match the invite">
    You have to use the exact address the invite was sent to. Capital letters
    don't matter. With Google, the Google account has to use that address. A
    mismatch doesn't use up the invite, so correct it and try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I never got the confirmation or sign-in email">
    Check spam, junk, or promotions. Look for a sender called OGC Deacons. On
    the **Confirm your email** screen you can press **Resend confirmation
    email**. For a sign-in link, request another one. Sign-in links expire
    after 15 minutes. If nothing arrives, tell the deacons. The problem is
    probably on our side.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I asked for a sign-in link but nothing came">
    For privacy, the screen says "If this email has an account, we've sent it a
    link" whether or not there's an account. Check that you used the address
    you registered with, and that you finished confirming your email after
    registering.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Google sign-in says there's no active account">
    Google only works for an account that is registered **and** confirmed, with
    the same email address as your Google account. Finish confirming your email
    first, or use the emailed sign-in link instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see a Google button">
    The site may not have Google sign-in turned on. Use **Email me a sign-in
    link** instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It signed me out in the middle of a request">
    Sessions end after 30 minutes without activity. Your request is still
    there: sign in again and submit it. If the sign-in link opened in a new
    tab, switch back to your original tab and it continues where you left off.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My name isn't in the dropdown">
    The staff list comes from the church's Notion roster. Ask the office to add
    you — see [Roster management](/operations/roster-management). The list
    updates on the next site rebuild, not instantly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Filling out the form

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I assign the request to myself?">
    No — you're automatically added as a **follower**, which is what gets you
    notifications. The assignee dropdown deliberately excludes you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to pick an assignee?">
    No. Leave it Unassigned and the deacons will triage it from the Inbox.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit a request after submitting?">
    Not from this form. Open the task in Asana from the confirmation link and
    edit or comment there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Error messages

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "We couldn't reach the assistant." | Network problem or the AI service is down. | Wait a moment and submit again. Your typing is preserved. |
| "The assistant took too long to respond." | Philip exceeded the time limit. | Submit again. |
| "We couldn't put your summary together." | Philip failed to produce a usable summary. | Add a bit more detail to the request and resubmit. |
| "Asana took too long to respond." | Asana was slow or unreachable. | Press Confirm again — no task was created. |
| "Too many requests." | Rate limit hit. | Wait about a minute. Repeated sign-in link requests are limited more strictly (5 per 10 minutes). |
| "Your session expired after 30 minutes of inactivity…" (on the sign-in screen) | You were inactive for 30 minutes, or your access was removed. | Sign in again, then resubmit. Your request is kept. |
| Attachment warning on the success screen | Task created, file upload failed. | Add the file directly in Asana. **Don't resubmit.** |

<Note>
  The rule of thumb: if you never reached the **confirmation screen**, no task
  was created and it's safe to try again. If you *did* reach it, the task exists
  — fix anything else in Asana rather than resubmitting.
</Note>

## Privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can see my request?">
    Anyone with access to the OGC Deacons Asana project. Your name and the
    email you signed in with are added to the bottom of the task description.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is anything stored in my browser?">
    Your name and email, so the form fills them in next time. Your sign-in is a
    secure cookie that ends when you sign out, close the browser, or have been
    inactive for 30 minutes. No request content is kept locally.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
