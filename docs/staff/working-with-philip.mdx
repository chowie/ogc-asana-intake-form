Philip is an AI assistant whose only job is making sure the deacons get a request
they can act on without chasing you down for details.

## What Philip does

- Reads your form submission.
- Asks **up to three** short, plain-language questions, one at a time.
- Writes a five-part summary for the deacons.

That's it. Philip does not create the task, email anyone, or make decisions about
your request. He drafts the summary; **you** approve it.

## What Philip asks about

He's looking for the four things a deacon needs before acting:

1. What is actually needed
2. Who it's for
3. What "done" looks like
4. Timing or constraints

If your **Request Details** already cover those, Philip skips the questions and
goes straight to the summary.

<Tip>
  Want fewer questions? Write two or three sentences in Request Details instead
  of one. "The nursery needs a new changing table before Easter — the current one
  is wobbly and the strap is broken" answers three of the four on its own.
</Tip>

## The three-question limit

Philip stops after three questions no matter what and writes the summary with
whatever he has. He will not loop forever.

The chat panel shows the count as you go, so you always know how many are left.

## Reviewing and editing

The summary panel appears at the end of the chat with **Confirm** and **Edit**.

- **Confirm** creates the Asana task. This is the only step that sends anything.
- **Edit** returns you to the form with your original answers intact. When you
  resubmit, Philip's question count resets and he starts over from your revised
  request.

<Note>
  Philip's summary is what the deacons read — it becomes the body of the Asana
  task, followed by your name and email. Your original free-text details are
  folded into it rather than pasted in verbatim, so read the summary before
  confirming.
</Note>

## Under the hood

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which model, and where does it run?">
    Claude Haiku 4.5, called server-side from the `chat` Netlify Function. The
    Anthropic API key never touches the browser.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my request used to train an AI?">
    No. Requests go to the Anthropic API for the length of the conversation only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if Philip doesn't respond?">
    The request times out after about nine seconds and you get an error message
    with the form still editable. Try again — see the
    [FAQ](/staff/faq).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
