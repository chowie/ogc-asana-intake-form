Your profile holds two things: the **name** the deacons see on your requests,
and the **email** you sign in with. Open it any time with **Profile** at the
top right of the page.

## Checking your name

The first time you sign in, before the request form appears, you'll see
**Welcome! Please check your details**. Your first and last name are already
filled in from your invitation, as the deacon typed them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the spelling">
    Fix anything that's wrong. Both names are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and continue">
    Press **Save and continue**. The request form opens, and your name appears
    at the top right (hover over it to see your email).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change it later, click **Profile**, edit the name, and press **Save**.
**Back to the request form** leaves without changing anything.

<Note>
  Your name and email are added to every request you submit, at the bottom of
  the Asana task. There's nothing to fill in on the form itself.
</Note>

## Changing your sign-in email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the change">
    Click **Profile**, then **Use a different email address** under
    **Sign-in email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new address twice">
    Type it in **New email** and **Confirm new email**, then press **Send
    confirmation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve from your current inbox">
    We send a link to your **current** address. Open it and click **Yes, I
    requested this** on the page that opens. Your email hasn't changed yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm from your new inbox">
    We then send a second link to your **new** address. Open it and click
    **Confirm this address**. Each link works once and expires after **1
    hour**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    You're signed in with the new address. Use it whenever you sign in from now
    on. Any other browser where you were signed in is signed out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Two links, on purpose. The one to your current address proves the change
  came from you. The one to your new address proves the address is yours (and
  catches typos). If you get one of these emails and didn't ask for it, ignore
  it. Nothing changes unless both links are clicked.
</Tip>

### When you can't change it here

- **You sign in with Google.** Your email follows your Google account, so the
  Profile page doesn't offer a change.
- **You're a deacon who sends invites.** Those addresses are set in the site
  configuration. Ask the site administrator to change it.
- **"That email address is already in use."** Another account already uses
  the new address. Choose a different one, or ask a deacon for help.
- **You no longer have access to your current inbox.** The change has to be
  approved from there. Ask a deacon to update your account.
- **The second link never arrived.** Check the new address's spam folder. If
  it still isn't there (or it expired), start the change again from
  **Profile**.
